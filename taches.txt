## Configuration Ollama

Si tu as moins de 16 GB de RAM/VRAM, limite Ollama a 1 modele charge :

**macOS :**
    launchctl setenv OLLAMA_MAX_LOADED_MODELS 1
    # puis redemarrer Ollama

**Linux :**
    sudo systemctl edit ollama.service
    # ajouter : Environment="OLLAMA_MAX_LOADED_MODELS=1"

**Windows :**
    setx OLLAMA_MAX_LOADED_MODELS 1
